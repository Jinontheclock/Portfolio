    How adoption works

    1. 1

      Meet your match

      Browse the cats, take the match quiz, or visit an adoption day. Each one has a story and is looking for the right home.

    2. 2

      Fill out an application

      A quick online form about your lifestyle and home, so we can make sure the fit is right — for you and the cat.

    3. 3

      Welcome home

      A short trial stay, then the paperwork — and a lifetime of tiny paws on your keyboard.

    Why we do what we do

    Every cat deserves to feel safe, loved, and at home. We step in when they've been abandoned, forgotten, or born without a place to go — medical care first, then a foster sofa, then a family.

    It's not always easy. But every soft purr, headbutt, and happy tail makes it worth it.

    You've helped us save…

    Behind every number is a little life that got a fresh start — and it's all thanks to fosters, adopters, donors, and people who simply told a friend about us. We're just getting started.
