    About us

    A volunteer-run kitten rescue in Vancouver — no shelter building, just people who can't walk past a cardboard box that meows.

    Our Story

    At TinyPaws, we believe every cat deserves a chance at a safe, loving life. We are a dedicated non-profit rescue focused on saving abandoned, orphaned, and at-risk kittens, providing them with medical care, nourishment, and a warm foster home until they're ready for adoption.

    Beyond rescue work, TinyPaws is committed to reducing feline homelessness through community education, affordable spay/neuter initiatives, and adoption events that match our furry friends with loving forever homes.

    Each tiny paw makes a big imprint on our hearts—join us in our mission to ensure every kitten has the opportunity to purr, play, and thrive in a safe and loving environment.

    Our Mission

    Rescue & Rehabilitate

    We provide immediate care and shelter to stray, abandoned, and at-risk cats, ensuring they receive the medical attention, safety, and love they need to heal.

    Foster-based support

    Every cat in our care is placed in a loving foster home, where they can thrive emotionally and physically until they're ready for adoption.

    Forever Home

    We match each cat with a carefully chosen forever family to ensure a lifelong, loving bond.

    Community Education

    We advocate for responsible cat ownership, including spaying/neutering, proper care, and understanding feline behavior.

    Lifelong Advocacy

    From kittens to seniors, we stand by every cat's right to a safe, healthy, and happy life—no matter their age or background.

    How the rescue actually runs

    TinyPaws started the way most rescues do: one litter too many, and a group of neighbours who couldn't say no. What began as a spare bathroom and a group chat grew into a foster network across the city — every placement, vet run, and adoption day handled by volunteers.

    We still have no building, and we like it that way. Cats don't learn to live in homes by living in cages — every TinyPaws kitten grows up on someone's sofa, learning stairs, vacuum cleaners, and laps, which is exactly the life they're headed to next.
