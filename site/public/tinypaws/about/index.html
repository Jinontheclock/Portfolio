About us

A volunteer-run kitten rescue in Vancouver — no shelter building, just people who can't walk past a cardboard box that meows.

Our story

TinyPaws started the way most rescues do: one litter too many, and a group of neighbours who couldn't say no. What began as a spare bathroom and a group chat grew into a foster network across the city.

We still have no building, and we like it that way. Cats don't learn to live in homes by living in cages — every TinyPaws kitten grows up on someone's sofa, which is exactly where they're headed next.

Our mission

Rescue

Kittens from tough starts — strays, surrenders, and litters born outside.

Foster

No shelter building — every cat grows up in a real home, learning to be someone's cat.

Match

Adoptions built on fit, not speed — so the home a cat lands in is the last one.
