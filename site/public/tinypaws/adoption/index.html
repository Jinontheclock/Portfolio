    How adoption works

    Five steps, no surprises. The fee ($240) covers vaccines, spay or neuter, and a microchip.

    The five steps

    1. Step 1: Browse & meet

      Look through the adoptable cats, take the match quiz, or meet kittens at an adoption day. Bonded pairs go home together.

    2. Step 2: Submit an application

      Fill in the application below. It isn't first-come-first-served — we match on fit, not speed.

    3. Step 3: Chat with a volunteer

      A foster volunteer calls you within a few days to talk lifestyle, home setup, and any other pets.

    4. Step 4: Trial stay

      A one-week trial at your home with supplies provided, so everyone can be sure — including the cat.

    5. Step 5: Bring them home

      Sign the adoption agreement and pay the fee ($240, covering vaccines, spay/neuter, and microchip).
